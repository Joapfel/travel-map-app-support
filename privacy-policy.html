Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Travel Map App ("the app") is an offline-first travel photo atlas. We designed it so that your photos and travel history never leave your device. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not do with your information.

Data we collect

None. Travel Map App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics SDKs, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

Photo library access

With your permission, the app reads photos from your device's photo library to place them on your private travel map. For each photo it uses only the embedded location (GPS) coordinates and the date the photo was taken. All of this processing happens locally on your device. Your photos and their metadata are never uploaded to any server.

Where your data is stored

The travel map, places, and counts the app builds from your photos are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. Deleting the app removes this data. Nothing is backed up to our servers because we do not operate any servers that receive your data.

Location lookup

To turn GPS coordinates into readable place names, the app uses only its local cache. If a place is not already cached, the app shows the coordinates without contacting a geocoding service. It does not continuously track your location and does not use your device's live location services.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
